The book was revised after its first capture looked flat: layered pages, gilded bindings, a recessed mouth, two fang rows, eyes and cover ornament now provide depth. Its [closed pose](book-closed.png) shows the binding and cover. Teeth use gums, tongue and a dark throat; the computer has a visible keyboard, hinged screen and animated face. Flippers have mechanical springs and a grounded pressure plate. The hydrant has flanges, fasteners and chains, with an [active water jet](hydrant-active.png).

The burning section has drums, grating, rails and a supported asphalt launch ramp. The final capture confirms support posts stay below the ramp; rendered ramp position and rotation now share the physical definition. Water has animated caustics, deep walls, pipe rails and a boost approach. Its rim meets the ground without duplicate top faces. Potions have shaped glass, distinct liquids, corks and bubbles. Materials remain deliberately stylized and less worn than the reference; water and gums are the weakest material treatments. GPU performance on target hardware remains unmeasured.

The [Inferno pit capture](inferno-pit.png) shows stone-lined shafts with lava 4.2 metres below ground and the enlarged 14pt readouts.
