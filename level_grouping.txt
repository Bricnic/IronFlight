Suggested Official Level Groups
===============================

Each official level has one primary group, chosen by its scenery, props and
overall theme. Levels that blend themes can also receive secondary tags.
Race, collection and demolition modes should remain separate filters.

1. Sweet Celebrations (8 levels)
-------------------------------
Birthday parties and candy worlds, featuring cakes, candles, sweets and
colorful decorations.

- Birthday Cake Circuit
- Birthday Table Loop
- Bouncy House
- Gumdrop Meadows
- Lollipop Loop
- Peppermint Pass
- Chocolate Chasm
- Sugar Rush Summit

2. Winter Wonderland (6 levels)
-------------------------------
Christmas villages, frozen lakes and snowy mountain courses. Frostfire Pass
belongs here for its alpine setting and snowman, despite its fire hazards.

- Tinsel Town
- Mistletoe Mile
- Nutcracker Run
- Sleigh Bell Sprint
- North Pole Summit
- Frostfire Pass

3. Spooky Places (4 levels)
--------------------------
Haunted landscapes and dungeon environments, with ghosts, gravestones,
skulls, Halloween decorations and sinister creatures.

- Dungeon
- Haunted Hollow
- Pumpkin Pass
- Spider Hollow

4. Toybox Adventures (10 levels)
--------------------------------
Oversized toys, household objects and tabletop obstacle courses. Dinosaur
levels fit here as adventures among giant toy creatures and colored tracks.

- Block Party
- Toybox Road
- Tabletop Brawl
- Fireside Sonata
- Booster Gap
- Rock and Roll
- Screwball Run
- Jurassic Junction
- Emberfall Causeway
- Chompworks

5. Stunt Parks (3 levels)
-------------------------
Purpose-built stunt and amusement parks centered on loops, rings, rides and
arena attractions.

- Triple Loop
- Fairground
- Ring Chase

6. Industrial Mayhem (10 levels)
--------------------------------
Machinery, hazardous infrastructure and urban environments. This group
combines industrial fire, lava and slime courses with city, scrapyard and
waterfront settings.

- Foundry Run
- Inferno Vault
- Machine Gauntlet
- Amber Undertow
- Evil Tube Run
- Slime Surge
- Volcano Loop
- Skyline Circuit
- Scrapyard Sweep
- Shipyard

7. Outdoor Adventures (3 levels)
--------------------------------
Courses defined by natural terrain and traversal: canyon drops, mountain
rapids, desert obstacles and water crossings.

- Canyon Plunge
- Sky Slide
- Sharkbite Run

Total: 44 official levels across 7 groups.
